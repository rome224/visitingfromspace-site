Broadcast Day

What your Fox News broadcast day looks like

Here's what to expect if your product is booked for a live shot. Please read it closely and reach out with any questions.

When do we arrive and where do we enter?
Plan to arrive between 4:00am and 4:15am. You'll come in through the after-hours entrance at the LVCC Central Hall. We'll send you a map with the entrance marked so it's easy to find.
How do we get in that early?
Everyone on your team needs a special after-hours wristband to get into the building. No one can get in without one. If you're attending Unveiled, Pepcom, or ShowStoppers before your broadcast day, the Fox team can get your wristbands to you there. If not, they'll meet you at the after-hours entrance the morning of your broadcast.
Where do we go once we're inside?
Head to the Central Broadcast Tower. Note this is different from the Central Broadcast Center. The Fox set is at the Broadcast Tower, first floor, corner stage. Coming from the direction you'll enter, their corner is all the way to the right. The map we send will show you the way.
What setup will they provide?
They provide a table and a power outlet. Anything else you need to demo your product well, please bring it yourself. That includes a Wi-Fi hotspot if your product needs internet. You must be fully set up no later than 4:45am so they can start broadcasting by 5:00am. There's a restroom nearby if your product needs water. Non-working or non-broadcast-ready samples may be set aside and left out of the broadcast.
Do we have to stay the whole time?
You don't have to, but we strongly recommend it. This is a multi-city broadcast, so your product could go live at different times. If anything goes wrong with your product during the show, no one will be available to fix it for you, and non-working items may be set aside and left out.
Will anyone from our company be on camera?
No. No one from your company appears on camera. We'll ask you for a few talking points ahead of time so we can share your message the right way.
When will we be free to leave?
The last broadcast wraps by 9:30am local time, and you'll be free to go by 10:00am at the latest.
Will our sample be returned if we leave it there?
No. They leave it where it is, and they can't guarantee it will still be there when you come back.
