What Great Press Does

Here are five ways real news coverage grows your business.

Benefit 01
01

Reach Millions of People

Instantly connect with millions, boosting your brand's visibility and impact across the globe.

One story in a major news outlet can put your company in front of millions of readers, viewers and listeners in a single day. And it doesn't stop there. Great stories get picked up, shared and repeated by other reporters, blogs and social media, spreading your name to audiences you could never reach on your own.

Imagine your product on the homepage of Yahoo News, featured on CNBC and talked about on TechCrunch, all in the same week. That's the kind of reach our clients get, and it's the kind of reach we work hard to get for you.

Benefit 02
02

Instant Sales Boost

Fasten your seatbelts. Reporters are always looking for companies to feature, so press coverage can happen super fast. Just one online write-up, one TV interview, or one magazine mention can expose your company to thousands, even millions, of targeted consumers.

This surge of interest can lead to more people buying from you right away. Great press is like a powerful spotlight that shines on your business, driving sales up fast!

Benefit 03
03

Build Trust

When you're featured in the news, it boosts your credibility and shows that others find your products or services valuable. Press coverage also improves your online presence. When people search for your business on Google, they'll find positive articles and stories.

Seeing great news features online makes people feel more confident and trusting in your business. This combination of trusted news and a strong online presence can make a big difference in how people view your company.

Benefit 04
04

Leave Your Competitors in the Dust

Press will give you a competitive advantage. When your business is featured in the news and your competitors are not, more people will know about you. This attention will make customers choose your products or services over others.

Being in the spotlight makes your business look special, giving you a big edge over competitors!

Benefit 05
05

Attract Investors

Positive press can attract investors who are looking for new businesses to support. When your company is featured in the media, it shows that people are interested in what you do.

Investors often read the news to find the next big opportunity. Seeing your business mentioned makes them think you are a good choice for investment, which can help you grow even more!
