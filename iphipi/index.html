Features

What makes IPHIPI different

1. It knows the moment

It understands what's being said, the sounds around you, where you are and the time of day. It learns your routines, so its help fits the moment.

2. It acts before you ask

It hears the plans and promises you mention and acts on them, so you stop carrying a to-do list in your head.

3. Your voice is the interface

Talk naturally while you walk, drive, cook or hold a toddler. No screen, no app, no typing.

4. It has manners

It stays quiet in meetings, at dinner and at bedtime, and speaks up only when it's useful. It always asks before it spends your money.

5. Privacy by design

Wearer-only listening, conversation mode with permission, or listening off with one phrase. Raw recordings are never stored, and your data stays on your phone.

6. An agent for every part of your day

It drafts follow-ups at work, reorders essentials at home, translates when you travel and counts reps at the gym.

Our story

It started with callers who couldn't hear

We started IPHIPI convinced that edge AI would change everyday devices. India showed us where to start.

Earbuds were selling like hot cakes there. But in one of the noisiest countries in the world, everyone had the same complaint. Callers couldn't hear them. So that became our first mission. We built an ultra-low-power AI chip into earbuds to cut out the noise and make them truly hands-free.

Then, in 2026, AI agents arrived. Suddenly, earbuds, glasses and other wearables became the ears and eyes those agents needed. Most AI still lives on a screen, but nurses, drivers, shop owners and parents have full hands and no free screen.

Today, we're a team of about 30 across Singapore, where we're headquartered, Shenzhen and Hyderabad, where IPHIPI began in 2023. We've been building quietly, in stealth.

At CES 2027, we're launching the IPHIPI Agentic AI Platform to the world for the first time.
