The problem

Right now, your AI only works
when your phone is in your hand.

Today’s AI lives in apps, chats and browsers. It only helps when you stop, unlock your phone and type. But most of the moments that matter happen when your hands are full.

The moment
“I’ll send the quote by Friday.”
AI on your phoneHears nothing. You remember on Friday afternoon, too late.
IPHIPIDrafts the email, blocks time on Thursday and nudges you before the deadline.
The moment
Over drinks, a friend says, “You have to try Burma Superstar on Clement. Get the tea leaf salad.”
AI on your phoneHears nothing. By morning, you’ve forgotten the name.
IPHIPISaves the place and the dish. Next time you’re nearby at dinner time, it reminds you, and books a table if you say yes.
The moment
“Pick the kids up at 4, not 5, and we’re out of milk.”
AI on your phoneHears nothing. You turn up at 5 and get home without milk.
IPHIPIUpdates the pickup time, adds milk to the list and reminds you on the way home.

The solution

It hears what you say you’ll do,
then does it.

Just talk like you always do. IPHIPI takes care of the rest. It writes the email, puts it on your calendar, adds it to your list or reminds you later. You just say “yes.”

01

Keeps your promises

It turns what you say into reminders, emails and calendar events.

02

Does tasks for you

Ask it to answer a text or reorder your groceries. It writes the text the way you talk and orders the groceries you usually buy. It always checks with you before it spends money.

03

Knows the right moment

It speaks up when it’s useful and stays quiet in meetings or at dinner.

04

Gets to know you

Your usual orders, your addresses and how you write. “Order my usual” just works.

05

Hears through noise

Even on a loud train, it can hear you whisper.

06

Protects your privacy

Your data stays on your phone. Your passwords and cards are locked away where the AI can’t see them. Recordings of your voice are never saved.

How it works, in four steps.

1

Put them on

Get earbuds or glasses with IPHIPI built in. Pair them with your phone and open the Action Agent app.

2

Say hello

Say its name to turn it on. Then choose your privacy setting and connect your calendar, email and shopping apps.

3

Live your day

Just talk like normal. Say “I’ll send it Friday” or “We’re out of milk,” and it takes care of it. Say “yes” to confirm.

4

Get your recap

Each evening, it sums up what got done and what still needs your OK.

Our story

It started with callers who couldn’t hear.

We started IPHIPI in 2023, convinced that AI running right on small devices would change everyday products. India showed us where to start.

Earbuds were selling like hot cakes there. But in one of the noisiest countries in the world, everyone had the same complaint. Callers couldn’t hear them. So we built an ultra-low-power AI chip into earbuds to cut out the noise and make them truly hands-free.

Then AI assistants arrived, and the team saw the next step. Most AI only helps people sitting at a screen. IPHIPI set out to help everyone else, the people whose hands are full and whose phones are in their pockets.
